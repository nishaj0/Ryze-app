# Community splits and your library

## Your split library

Open **Profile → Switch Split** to see the programs you have saved. The active program is marked **Active**. Saving or creating another program does not change your active program; open its details and choose **Activate split** when you are ready.

- Create a custom plan or edit a prebuilt plan to add a private plan to your library.
- Remove a non-active entry from the library. Removing a plan you created also deletes that private plan; removing another plan only removes your saved copy.
- You must activate another plan before removing the active one.

## Sharing your plan

For an original plan you created, use the share control in the library to publish it to the community. A plan can be published only when every training (non-rest) day has at least one exercise. Unpublish it at any time to remove it from community browsing.

Prebuilt programs and community copies cannot be published. This keeps authorship clear and prevents a copied plan from changing the community original.

## Finding community programs

Open **Profile → Community Splits** to browse community programs. You can:

- Sort by recent, popular, or most saved.
- Filter by plan type.
- Open a card to see its full training days and exercises.
- Like a program. Likes are tied to your account and can be toggled.
- Save a private copy. The copy includes all days, exercises, sets, reps, and notes, but is independent from the original.

Saving a community program never switches your active split. The only exception is during onboarding: choosing **Save & start** saves the copy and makes it your first active split.

## What happens to copies

Your saved community plan is a fork: you can edit it freely without changing the author’s plan, and later changes by the author will not change yours. If an author unpublishes their plan, your existing private copy remains available.
